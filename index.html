01 / COMPUTATIONAL MODELLING

Computational Fluid Dynamics of an Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm

This was a project I worked on during my BSc undergrad in UCD. It took a CFD investigation of haemodynamic behaviour within an idealised abdominal aortic aneurysm using ANSYS Fluent. Steady and pulsatile inlet conditions were compared to investigate velocity fields, recirculation and wall shear stress.
