02 / Biomedical Electronics

Analogue ECG Acquisition & Signal Conditioning

Design, construction and testing of a three-electrode analogue ECG acquisition circuit for measuring and conditioning cardiac electrical signals.

Analogue Electronics ECG Signal Conditioning MATLAB

01

Overview

How can a low-amplitude cardiac signal be acquired and conditioned using analogue circuitry?

This project involved the design and construction of a three-electrode analogue ECG acquisition circuit capable of amplifying the millivolt-level electrical activity produced by the heart.

The circuit combined amplification and analogue filtering before being progressively tested using known input signals. Following bench validation, the completed system was used to record an ECG signal from the body for further analysis and digital signal processing.

02

Engineering Approach

Acquisition

Three-electrode ECG

Surface electrodes were used to acquire the small differential electrical signal generated by cardiac activity. The analogue front end was designed to amplify this signal while reducing unwanted interference.

Signal Conditioning

Amplification & filtering

The circuit incorporated amplification and filtering stages designed around the frequency range of interest for ECG acquisition.

Target passband: 0.5–100 Hz
Design gain: 625 V/V
Measured mid-band gain: 640.7 V/V

Circuit Construction

From circuit design to physical implementation

The ECG acquisition system was constructed on a breadboard and tested progressively rather than only evaluating the completed circuit.
